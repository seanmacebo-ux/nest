My read

Cheapest step-free near work: Sydenham (R10,000) — ground garden unit, ~R2–3k Uber. Orange Grove is cheaper (R8,500) but the photos show a staircase up to the door, so I'd skip it for a ground-floor move. Norwood GV and Flex on Murray (Waverley) are both photo-confirmed step-free.

East: the Bedfordview garden unit (R12,500, ground floor) is upmarket at ~R4–5k Uber.

North (Fourways / Bryanston / Morningside): real ground-floor 2-beds from R10,500, but ~R5–9k+ Uber to Glenhazel. Only worth it once the licence and car are sorted.

Two things: listings sell fast (3 dropped off this list in a day), and use Bolt (10–20% cheaper than Uber).

👵 For Mom — cheap + ground floor, close by

Straight talk: the truly cheap bachelors (R1,700–R2,500) sit in inner-city Yeoville and Bellevue. They're close to the near-work suburbs but rougher on safety, which for your mom matters more than saving a few hundred rand. Kensington and Bez Valley are a safer step up for a bit more. Like the flats above, none of these ads state the floor, so it's one call first: "ground floor, no stairs to the door?" before viewing. Cheapest first.
