💰 The total-cost reality — read this before you fall for one

Almost everything you both picked sits far north (Sandton / Fourways / Rivonia / Randburg). The rent looks great — R10–15k — but car-less, the Uber to work runs R8k+/mo, so the real monthly cost lands at R18k–R23k. That's the number the budget actually feels.

Compare that to the cheap-Uber picks up top: a Sandringham ground-floor 2-bed at R11,000 + ~R2–3k Uber ≈ R13–14k true cost — roughly R6–9k/month cheaper than the far-north favourites, for the same 2 beds. Over a 12-month lease that's R70k–R100k difference, basically your whole car deposit.

My honest take: the far-north places become great after the car (Q2 2027). Right now, with a newborn and no car, the move that protects the budget is ground-floor + cheap Uber + close to work — Bedfordview Morninghill is the one far pick that makes sense (ground + garden + only mid Uber ≈ R18k). If you love Rivonia/Lonehill, they're worth it — just go in with eyes open that it's a ~R21–23k/mo lifestyle until you're driving.

Also remember the upfront: most ask 1 month deposit + 1 month rent to move in (≈ R26k on a R13k place), plus utilities usually excluded (~R1.5–2.5k/mo on top) unless the listing says included. Uber bands are estimates — use Bolt, it's 10–20% cheaper.
