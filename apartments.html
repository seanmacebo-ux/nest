Feng shui rules we follow (architect Cliff Tan, @dearmodern, from his interviews):

1. Bed: head on a solid wall, never the wall shared with a toilet. You can see the door from bed, but you're not in line with it. No mirror facing the bed.
2. Cot: same logic. Solid inside wall, out of the door line, not under the window. The bedroom with its own patio slider is the office / Mom's room, not the nursery.
3. Couch: back to a solid wall, front door to your side, never behind you. Cassini (3.2m) fits; lie along the 3m back row (Sean is 196cm, the 1.8m daybeds are too short).
4. Desk: next to a window so daylight hits your face, back to a wall.
5. Entry: keep it clear. If the hob is visible from the front door, a tall plant breaks the view.
6. Ground floor: curtains on both sliders, for privacy and security, and as Tan's fix for a bed near a window.

💰 The total-cost reality — read this before you fall for one

Rent is only half the story while you're car-less. The Uber to work runs R4–6k/mo from the East and R8k+/mo from Sandton, so a R13–14k place in Rivonia really costs ~R21k/mo, while the same rent in Edenvale/Greenstone lands ~R18k. That gap over a 12-month lease is basically your car deposit.

My honest take: with a newborn and no car, the budget-protecting move is East + step-free + close-ish to work. The Sandton places get great after the car (~Q2 2027). Remember the upfront too: most want 1 month deposit + 1 month rent (≈ R26k on a R13k place), plus utilities usually excluded (~R1.5–2.5k/mo) unless stated. Use Bolt, it's 10–20% cheaper than Uber.
