My read — balancing the place vs the Uber

Cheapest all-in (until the car): the near-work modern estates — Norwood Garden Village (R12k, confirmed ground floor) and Highlands North. ~R2–3k Uber.

Best of the East: Bedfordview — genuinely good modern complexes from R9,500, ~R4–5k Uber (fits your budget), and it's upmarket + close-ish to Greenstone if you like the area.

North (Bryanston / Fourways): lovely lifestyle estates from ~R10,850, but ~R9k+/month Uber to Glenhazel — only makes sense once your guy sorts the licence and the car's in. If the car comes fast, the whole North opens up.

Verify floor level + availability on every listing, and use Bolt (10–20% cheaper than Uber).
