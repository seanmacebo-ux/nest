🌀 Bedroom fan (29 Oct): not bought yet, not in the total

The honest bit: no fan cools the air. It cools you, by moving air over your skin. "Hot wind" happens when a weak fan pushes warm room air around. So pick a fan that moves a lot of air quietly. A 40cm pedestal beats a tower for that.

Make it actually help: (1) Daytime: windows and curtains shut so the heat stays out. (2) Evening, once it's cooler outside: put the fan in the window facing IN, to pull the cool night air through. (3) Hot night: a frozen 2L bottle in front of the fan gives a cooler breeze for an hour or so.

Skipped: air coolers (most on Takealot rate under 4★, and they work badly in Joburg's humid storm season). A real aircon is the only thing that truly cools the room, but a portable one is R5k+.
