🌀 Bedroom fan (29 Oct): 14 options under R750 (Takealot + Amazon), not bought yet

The honest bit: no fan cools the air. It cools you, by moving air over your skin. "Hot wind" happens when a weak fan pushes warm room air around. So pick a fan that moves a lot of air quietly. Babybean read the reviews on ~20 fans (up to 30 each) and counted how many say quiet, noisy, powerful or broke. Ranked best first. Skipped: Digimark (11 of 30 say noisy), ALVA (blades hitting the grill), the cheap 2-packs (noisy, die). Best-reviewed fan overall is the Salton High Velocity 40cm (4.7★, 391), but it's R841. Amazon (stealth browser, 29 Oct): fan reviews there are thin (most have under 15). The Kenwood and Salton are out of stock on Amazon. The 4 Amazon cards at the end are the only ones worth it.

Make it actually help: (1) Daytime: windows and curtains shut so the heat stays out. (2) Evening, once it's cooler outside: put the fan in the window facing IN, to pull the cool night air through. (3) Hot night: a frozen 2L bottle in front of the fan gives a cooler breeze for an hour or so.
