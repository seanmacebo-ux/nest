💛 What she needs

Bleeding usually lasts 2 to 6 weeks. Prices checked on Clicks and Dis-Chem, 11 Nov.

  • ✅Pads: got them (11 Nov) When they run low: Carriwell Premium Organic 10 pack, Clicks R62.99, or Lil-lets Maternity for nights, Clicks R59.99.done
  • ✅Takealot order: arrived (14 Nov) Tucks Medicated Cooling Pads, 100 (R370) · FertilitySure Perineal Healing Foam, 150ml (R249) · Vitality Aloe Vera Gel, 100ml (R79). Order 230236048. These are her cooling pads, no separate cold pack needed. Keep the tub in the fridge and lay one or two on top of her maternity pad.R698
  • 🍼Stopping her milk Medicine (Dr Moore, 13 Nov): Dostinex 0.5mg: half a tablet twice a day for 2 days (stand up slowly, it can cause dizziness). Vitamin B6 200mg every 8 hours for 14 days: with 100mg tablets that's 2 tablets 3 times a day, e.g. 7am, 3pm, 11pm. Stop and call Dr Moore if her hands or feet tingle or go numb. Prices: she needs 2 Dostinex tablets in total, so the 2-pack: up to ~R597 (R440.45 medicine + up to R156.94 pharmacy fee, the legal maximum on medicineprices.org.za). The generic Arigoline 0.5mg 2-pack is up to ~R362, so ask the pharmacist about swapping. B6: Gold Vitamin B6 100mg, 60 tablets, Dis-Chem R99.99 (R89.99 online, checked 14 Nov). She needs 84 tablets, so 2 tubs (~R180–200). Shower: let warm water ease the pressure and a little drip out, but don't squeeze or massage it empty, or her body makes more. Cabbage: cold leaves from the fridge, vein crushed, inside the bra around the breast, swapped when limp (about every 2 hours). Firm bra with no underwire, day and night. Panado for pain. Usually settles in a few days to a week. Doctor if: a hard, red, hot patch, fever or chills, or pain getting worse after a few days.~R800
  • ✅Maternity pants: got them (14 Nov) To hold her pads in place. If she needs more: Carriwell, Dis-Chem R82.99 (S–XL) or Clicks R91.20 (XXL).done
  • 💊Pain relief + a stool softener Whatever the hospital said is OK. Movicol sachets, Clicks R96.99 for 8. Tell the pharmacist she just gave birth.—
  • 🥤Water, iron-rich food, cranberry + coconut water She's lost blood, so red meat, spinach, beans and eggs help.—

Support for her heart, too: The Compassionate Friends (Highlands North), free one-on-one support from parents who've lost a child, plus a group on the second Saturday of every month. 011 440 6322 or 072 667 0273.

Back to hospital if: she soaks a pad in an hour or less, passes big clots, gets a fever or shivers, has smelly discharge or bad belly pain, a painful swollen leg, chest pain or short breath, or feels very low or unsafe.
