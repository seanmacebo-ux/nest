💻 Mishka's streaming laptop

For Whatnot live selling with OBS, Chrome and Spotify. Budget: R10k or less, pre-owned, a laptop, no Gumtree. Two spec sheets came through: a minimum and a recommended. The minimum is what makes R10k doable.

The minimum is for basic 720p at 30fps streaming. NVIDIA matters because its graphics cards have a built-in video encoder, so the stream doesn't load up the processor.

🎯 What to hunt for at R10k or less
  • A used "gaming" laptop with an RTX 3050, RTX 2050, GTX 1650 or GTX 1660 Ti. All of them clear the GTX 1050 minimum. Common models: ASUS TUF F15, Dell G15, MSI GF63 / Thin, Acer Nitro 5, HP Victus, Lenovo IdeaPad Gaming / LOQ.
  • Processor: Intel i5 or i7, 8th gen or newer (a model number ending in H is the faster laptop kind), or Ryzen 5 / 7.
  • 16GB RAM ideally. 8GB passes, and a second stick to make 16GB is a cheap upgrade later.
  • Ethernet port: gaming laptops almost always have one. If not, a USB Ethernet adapter is ~R200.
⚠️ Before you pay for a used one (tap to tick)
  • Check the graphics card is really there Task Manager → Performance → GPU. It must say NVIDIA and match the listing. The graphics card sets most of the price.
  • Run something heavy for 10 minutes A game or a test stream. Loud fans are normal. A keyboard too hot to touch, or stutter as it warms up, isn't.
  • Full-power charger included Gaming laptops need their own big charger to run at full speed.
  • Test every USB port + the Ethernet port Plug in a phone or mouse, and a network cable if the shop has one.
  • No BIOS password or old company account Restart it and make sure it boots straight into Windows with no lock screen from a previous owner.
  • Warranty written on the slip And keep the receipt.
