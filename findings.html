💻 Mini PC: import from GMKtec vs buy in SA

Finding: only the M6 Ultra comes out cheaper to import. The bigger ones cost the same or more once VAT is added.

The maths: landed price = (US$ price × R16.94) + import VAT + about R300 courier fee. SARS charges VAT on the customs value plus 10%, at 15%, so the VAT is 16.5% of the price (15% × 1.10). Computers are normally duty-free, so no duty is added.

💧 Weddell refills: Desertcart direct vs Amazon.co.za

Finding: Desertcart's own prices look cheaper (3+1 pack R2,543 vs R2,641 on Amazon), but that's probably not the full price. Amazon's R2,641 is all-in.

  • Desertcart's shipping policy adds shipping at checkout. It lists the countries where Desertcart pays customs, and South Africa isn't one of them, even though the site banner says "customs included".
  • If SARS VAT applies: R2,543 × 16.5% ≈ R420 VAT at the door. That makes it ≈ R2,963 + shipping, which is more than Amazon.
  • Amazon.co.za: VAT and delivery are included, and Amazon's A-to-z Guarantee covers it. It's sold by DesertcartZA, arriving 1–4 Dec.
  • Price per filter: genuine 3+1 = R2,641 ÷ 4 = R660 · genuine 2+2 = R2,346 ÷ 4 = R587 · compatible BFLEPATI 2+2 = R1,378 ÷ 4 = R345, plus shipping and possibly VAT. Compatible filters aren't NSF-certified.

Couldn't check: Desertcart's product pages block bots, even in real Chrome, so the actual checkout total is unknown. Test it on your phone, with the Edenvale address.
Sources: Desertcart shipping policy · Desertcart returns (store credit only) · Amazon 3+1 listing · SARS import VAT · options on the Bathroom page.

📽️ Projector that sits at the wall and copes with light

Finding: that's an ultra-short throw (UST) laser projector. The cheapest on Takealot is the Hisense PL2 at R27,498 (2,700 ANSI lumens). The brightest is the Epson LS650 at R40,729 (3,600 lumens).

  • Light: even 3,600 lumens washes out on a plain painted wall in daylight. A grey light-rejecting (ALR) screen is what makes UST work with light in the room. Takealot doesn't sell one.
  • Honest comparison: a Hisense 85" E7Q TV is R14,999 (4.7★, 23 reviews). That's about half the PL2, and better in a bright room.

Sources: brightness and prices from each Takealot listing (17 Nov). Options are on the Bedroom page.

🪟 Blinds for the bedroom window (no drilling)

Window: 145cm wide × 88cm tall inside the recess, from Sean's measuring photo (17 Nov).

  • Pleated paper blind (90 wide × 150 long): 145 ÷ 90 = 1.6, so you need 2. Trim each to about 72.5cm wide and 90cm long. 2 × R359 = R718.
  • Magnetic blackout (300 × 148): one 145 × 88 piece fits, with lots spare. R1,378 (was R952 this morning).
  • Stick-on blackout, size M (1.45m × 2m): the width matches exactly. Trim the length to about 90cm. R679.
  • Spring rod (110–200cm) fits 145. But the café curtains are 123cm long, which is 123 − 88 = 35cm too long.

🚿 Shower head: why 15cm

A wider head spreads the same water over a bigger area, so each jet is softer. The Weddell already lowers pressure a little, and Amazon's review summary mentions reduced pressure. So 15cm is the safe step up, and 20cm is the most to go for. All the options fit a standard ½-inch arm. Importing one isn't worth it: on a R100–R300 item, the shipping costs more than the head. Options are on the Bathroom page.

💧 Mineral water report: fact-check (17 Nov)

Sean shared a report on SA mineral waters. Each brand's numbers were checked against its own website with browser-kit.

Brand · what checks outVerdict
Aquamine (Cradle of Humankind, Gauteng)Ca 58, Mg 40, HCO₃ 240 match its site exactly. Also Na 5, K 4, Cl 8, SO₄ 12, silica 11. ⚠️ That analysis is from the CSIR, dated 10 Jan 2010. Glass bottles carry a deposit. Maths: the ions add up to 378 mg/L. Bicarbonate loses about half its weight when dried, so TDS ≈ 378 − 122 ≈ 256, which matches its "well above 250".✅ real, but old lab
Africqua Mount AquaCa 13.3, Mg 16.4, TDS 144 match its site. ⚠️ "97" is alkalinity as CaCO₃, not bicarbonate. As bicarbonate, it's about 97 × 1.22 ≈ 118 mg/L.✅ mostly right
Mountain Falls (Overberg)The TDS 345 and pH 7 are on its homepage. But its site doesn't publish the Ca 14, Mg 11 or HCO₃ 110 the report gives. If those were true, they'd only explain about a third of 345, so the rest would be other salts, like sodium and chloride. It's not a calcium/magnesium water. Glass: 12 × 750ml for R485.❓ ask for the lab sheet
