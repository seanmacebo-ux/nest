🛜 WiFi for the new place

Short version: take a 100 down / 50 up line, and spend on a good router. The router fixes the lag you feel, not the ISP.

📊 What your MWeb line is actually doing (test, 19 Oct)
Download / upload
103 / 53.8
Mbps on your phone over WiFi. That's the full 100/50 you pay R889 for.
Ping (idle)
5ms
Excellent.
Ping while uploading
234ms
This is the lag. It's called bufferbloat: when something uploads, the router queues everything else behind it.

What it means: MWeb is delivering the speed. The stutter on calls and games comes from the router. So whichever ISP you pick at Baker Rd, pair it with a router that has SQM (smart queue management, a setting that stops uploads from choking everything else).

⏳ Still to do: run the test at waveform.com/tools/bufferbloat on the laptop (on a cable if you can) and send Bean the grade. Then Bean prices 2 SQM routers.
