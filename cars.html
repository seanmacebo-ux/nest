How to use this + the honest trade

The one test that beats every rating: sit in the car with the seat all the way back to where you'd drive, then fit a rear-facing baby seat behind you. Knees clear + seat still fits = it passes. At 6'4 the brochure lies; your legs don't.

Japanese vs Chinese: Japanese = proven + best resale. Chinese = more car, tech and warranty for less, but softer resale and a shorter track record — the transferable warranties (Haval 7yr, Chery 5yr+) cover the baby years. Both smart; it's a priorities call.

Real-value standouts right now: Nissan Magnite (2025, from R219,900), MG ZS (2026, from R229,900), Chery Tiggo 4 Pro (from R229,900), and the cheap-runners Mazda2 / Swift / Starlet under R220k.
