Medical aid for both of you · researched 11 Nov 2026

Medical aid for us.

Cover for both of you, so a hospital, a specialist or a scan is never a question of money. Prices below are 2027 rates for two adults, per month. They start in January and still need sign-off from the Council for Medical Schemes (the regulator).

The short answer

  • Best value: Momentum Incentive (Associated hospitals), R6,489 a month for both of you.
  • Add gap cover (about R350–R500 a month). It pays what the medical aid doesn't when a specialist charges more than the scheme rate.
  • Join now, so cover starts 1 Dec or 1 Jan. Every new member waits before they're covered, so the sooner you start, the sooner it's done.
  • Mishka can add Sean as her partner on his passport, as long as his SA visa or permit is valid. Details below.

The plans

⭐ Best value
Momentum Incentive · Associated hospitals
R6,489 / month for both
  • Unlimited hospital cover at Momentum's Associated hospitals (incl. Life Roseacres, Brenthurst, Morningside).
  • Specialists paid up to double the scheme rate, so fewer surprise bills.
  • About R7,800 a year in a savings account for GP visits, scans and medicine.
  • Pregnancy: 12 check-ups, 2 scans, urine cultures, nurse visits after the birth.
Close second
Discovery Classic Smart
R5,828 / month for both
  • Unlimited hospital cover at Discovery's Smart network hospitals (incl. Roseacres, Park Lane, Linkwood).
  • Cheaper, but no savings account. Extra visits come out of your pocket.
  • Pregnancy: 8 check-ups and 2 scans.
  • ⚠️ Some procedures outside its day-surgery network cost R13,150 upfront.
Step-up
Momentum Incentive · Any hospital
R8,063 / month for both
  • Same plan as the top pick, but you can use any private hospital.
  • Worth it if the specialist you want delivers at a hospital that isn't on the Associated list.
❌ Avoid
Discovery Active Smart · Bonitas BonStart · KeyCare
  • Active Smart and BonStart cap cover for a newborn in intensive care at R60k–R75k. Neonatal ICU costs about R23,000 a day.
  • KeyCare and other plans priced by income: at your income you'd land in the top band (KeyCare Plus is R8,672 for two), so they stop being cheap.

👤 Adding Sean

  • Mishka joins as the main member and adds Sean as her "spouse or partner". Either of you can be the main member. It costs about the same either way.
  • Sean joins on his passport. Discovery's 2027 form takes an ID or passport number.
  • His SA visa or permit has to be valid: a work, spousal or relative's visa, or permanent residence. A visitor visa won't qualify. This comes from Google's summary, so confirm it with the scheme.
  • Not married? Schemes usually accept a partner, but they may ask for a sworn statement that you live together.

⏳ Waiting periods, in plain words

  • 3 months: new members with no previous medical aid wait up to 3 months before claims are paid.
  • Up to 12 months: the scheme can exclude anything you were treated for in the last 12 months, for up to a year.
  • Pregnancy: if you're already pregnant when you join, the scheme can refuse to pay for it. A pregnancy that starts after joining is covered. So join first, and get the waiting periods in writing before planning anything.
  • Answer every question on the form honestly. If you leave something out, they can cancel the cover later, exactly when you need it.

✅ Steps

  1. Check Sean's visa or permit and its expiry date This decides whether he can go on the plan.
  2. Talk to a broker, or apply directly A broker costs you nothing (the scheme pays them) and can confirm the hospital lists and waiting periods.
  3. Choose a gynae and a hospital, then the plan Pick the plan whose hospital list includes the hospital you want. Mediclinic Sandton says it cares for extremely premature babies, and Life Roseacres has a confirmed neonatal ICU.
  4. Apply, plus gap cover Turnberry Dynamic also pays a scheme's upfront hospital charges. Most gap cover waits 10–12 months before it pays for a pregnancy.
  5. Get the waiting periods in writing Keep the letter.
