Medical aid · updated 12 Nov 2026

Medical aid: Mishka first.

Mishka joins now on her own, so her waiting periods start. Sean is added the day his spousal visa comes through. Prices are 2027 rates for one adult, per month. They start in January and still need sign-off from the Council for Medical Schemes (the regulator).

The short answer

  • Top pick: Momentum Incentive (Associated hospitals), R3,627 a month. Its own specialists are paid in full in hospital, so there's no doctor shortfall even before gap cover kicks in.
  • Add gap cover the same day: Turnberry Dynamic, R190 a month if Mishka is under 30, R350 if 30–49. Starting both on the same day skips gap cover's 3-month general wait.
  • Join on 1 December if you can. December is charged at 2026 rates, and the waiting periods start a month sooner.
  • The real cost isn't the premium. It's the gynae's rooms. Gynaes charge R1,900–R2,400 a visit and schemes pay about R520. Gap cover doesn't pay rooms visits. Plan for R11–46k out of pocket in a pregnancy year on any plan.

📘 How to use it

Tap any card to open it.

🏥Hospital coverThe big pot · no limit

Pays the hospital bill, theatre and NICU (neonatal intensive care) with no annual limit.

The catch: only at a hospital on the plan's list. On Momentum that's the Associated list (Life Roseacres is on it). Anywhere else, you pay 30% of the bill.

👛Day-to-day moneyThe small pot · ~R4,356 a year

Pays GP visits, scans in the doctor's rooms and medicine. On Momentum it's paid in upfront for the whole year, and whatever's left over carries over to next year.

The catch: when it runs out, everything outside hospital comes from your pocket until the new year.

🛡️Gap coverThe top-up · R190–R350 a month

Separate insurance. In hospital, it pays what doctors charge above what the medical aid pays (up to 5× the scheme rate on Turnberry), plus some scheme co-payments.

The catch: it never pays for visits to the doctor's rooms. And it waits 10 months before it pays anything to do with a pregnancy.

🧾How a bill actually worksOne example, in rands

In the gynae's rooms: the visit costs R2,000. The medical aid pays about R520 (from the pregnancy benefit or savings). You pay about R1,480. Gap cover doesn't help here.

In hospital: the hospital bill is paid in full. The specialist charges R16,000 and the scheme pays R12,000 at double rate. Gap cover pays the R4,000 left over, once you claim it. With a Momentum Associated specialist, there's nothing left over at all.

✅ The habits that save the most

Tap each one for the how.

1Phone before anything plannedGet a pre-authorisation number

Before any planned hospital stay, procedure or MRI/CT scan, phone the scheme (the number is on the card and in the app) and get an approval number. That approval is called pre-authorisation.

Without it, they can pay less or charge a penalty (R500 on KeyHealth, 20% on some schemes).

Emergency? Go first. Phone within 1–2 working days.

2Stay on their listsNetwork hospitals and doctors

Use the app to find hospitals, GPs and specialists on the plan's network before you book.

Going outside it costs 30% of the hospital bill on Momentum (Associated), or R13,150 upfront on Discovery Smart plans.

3Ask every new doctor one questionIt decides most of your costs

Say: "Are you on Momentum's Associated list, and what will I pay myself per visit?"

Some gynaes bill the medical aid and charge only about R450 on top. Others charge R1,900–R2,400 cash. That's the difference between about R11k and R46k a year.

4Go easy on the day-to-day moneyIt has to last 12 months

Don't spend it on pharmacy extras (vitamins, creams, plasters) early in the year. Pay for small things in cash and save it for doctor visits and scans.

Use generic medicine when the pharmacist offers it. It's cheaper and does the same job.

5Claim from gap coverIt won't pay unless you ask

After any hospital stay, send gap cover:

  • each doctor's invoice
  • the medical aid's claim statement showing what it paid
  • the hospital account

Every policy has a deadline for claims. Check the exact one in the policy, and claim as soon as the medical aid's statement arrives.

6Check the app every monthCatch wrong claims early

Look at every claim. If a doctor billed for something that didn't happen, or the scheme paid less than it should, phone them.

If a doctor sends you a bill directly, submit it to the scheme yourself. Claims have to be in within 4 months, or they won't pay.

7Be honest on the formEvery question, every time

If something is left off the application form, the scheme can cancel the cover later, exactly when you need it.

Declare everything, and get any waiting periods or exclusions in writing.

🪤 Co-payments and excesses

What Mishka pays herself even with medical aid. Most are waived in a real emergency.

  • Wrong hospital: 30% of the bill on Momentum (Associated), KeyHealth, Bonitas and Medshield. R13,150 upfront on Discovery Smart plans. None on Momentum (Any), Discovery Classic Saver, Medihelp MedAdd, Bestmed Beat3, Fedhealth flexiFED.
  • MRI or CT, per scan: Discovery first R4,155 · Momentum R3,650 · Fedhealth R3,170 · Medihelp R2,200 · Bonitas R1,940 · KeyHealth R1,790 · Bestmed and Medshield none.
  • Day procedures in the wrong place: Discovery Classic Saver R7,550 · Bonitas R5,670 · Bestmed R2,987 if done in a full hospital instead of a day clinic.
  • Skipping pre-authorisation: R500 on KeyHealth, 20% on Medihelp and Medshield.
  • The rule that avoids nearly all of it: use a hospital and day clinic inside the plan's network, and get pre-authorisation first.

👤 Adding Sean later

  • When his spousal visa comes through, Mishka adds him as her "spouse or partner". He joins on his passport.
  • Cost for both of you, per month: Momentum Incentive (Associated) R6,489 · Discovery Classic Smart R5,828 · Momentum Incentive (Any hospital) R8,063. So about R2,860 more than Mishka alone.
  • A temporary visa probably won't qualify. Schemes generally want a work, spousal or relative's visa, or permanent residence. Ask the broker if any scheme takes him sooner.
  • Not married? Schemes usually accept a partner, but may ask for a sworn statement that you live together.
  • Avoid for a family later: Discovery Active Smart and Bonitas BonStart. They cap NICU at R60–75k, and NICU costs about R23,000 a day.

⏳ Waiting periods, in plain words

  • 3 months: new members with no previous medical aid wait up to 3 months before claims are paid.
  • Up to 12 months: the scheme can exclude anything she was treated for in the last 12 months, for up to a year. Expect them to ask about this year's pregnancy.
  • A future pregnancy that starts after joining is covered. Get the waiting periods in writing before planning anything.
  • Answer every question on the form honestly. If something's left out, they can cancel the cover later, exactly when you need it.
