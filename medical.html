Medical aid for us.

Cover for both of you, so a hospital, a specialist or a scan is never a question of money. Prices below are 2027 rates for two adults, per month. They start in January and still need sign-off from the Council for Medical Schemes (the regulator).

The short answer

  • Best value: Momentum Incentive (Associated hospitals), R6,489 a month for both of you.
  • Add gap cover (about R350–R500 a month). It pays what the medical aid doesn't when a specialist charges more than the scheme rate.
  • Join now, so cover starts 1 Dec or 1 Jan. Every new member waits before they're covered, so the sooner you start, the sooner it's done.
  • Mishka can add Sean as her partner on his passport, as long as his SA visa or permit is valid. Details below.

👩 Mishka on her own · 10 schemes checked (12 Nov)

2027 prices for one adult per month. We looked at pregnancy cover, GP visits and specialist visits.

  • The big lesson from the full cost check: every good plan pays hospital and NICU without limit. What costs you is the gynae's rooms: check-ups and scans. Doctors charge R1,900–R2,400 a visit, while schemes pay about R520. Expect R11k–R46k out of pocket in a pregnancy year on any plan. So: a cheap premium with unlimited hospital cover, plus gap cover.
  • Best balance: Momentum Incentive (Associated), R3,627. Associated specialists are paid in full in hospital, so there's no shortfall even before gap cover kicks in. 12 check-ups, no NICU limit. Pregnancy-year estimate: R69k–R91k. Traps: 30% outside Associated hospitals, R3,650 per MRI.
  • Best for everyday: Discovery Classic Smart Saver, R3,616. Unlimited network GP visits at R80 each, specialists paid up to double. Estimate: R68k–R90k. Trap: R13,150 upfront for a planned birth outside its network.
  • Cheapest: KeyHealth Origin, R2,842. Estimate: R60k–R82k, but only with gap cover, because it pays specialists just 100%. Thin everyday cover (R3,580/yr).
  • Gap cover: Turnberry Dynamic, R190/month if under 30 or R350 if 30–49. It covers up to 5× the scheme rate plus scheme co-payments. 10-month wait for pregnancy.
  • Dropped: CompCare (reserves 23.2%, below the legal 25%, and no elective C-sections) and Genesis MED-200 Plus (excludes pregnancy if a waiting period applied when joining). Also checked: Medihelp, Bonitas, Bestmed, Fedhealth and Medshield. They cost more each month for the same pregnancy cover.
  • Note: Genesis Clinic says it specialises in low-risk pregnancies and doesn't mention a neonatal ICU. Ask the new gynae where they'd deliver.

👤 Adding Sean

  • Mishka joins as the main member and adds Sean as her "spouse or partner". Either of you can be the main member. It costs about the same either way.
  • Sean joins on his passport. Discovery's 2027 form takes an ID or passport number.
  • His SA visa or permit has to be valid: a work, spousal or relative's visa, or permanent residence. A visitor visa won't qualify. This comes from Google's summary, so confirm it with the scheme.
  • Not married? Schemes usually accept a partner, but they may ask for a sworn statement that you live together.
  • Our plan (11 Nov): Mishka joins now on her own, so her waiting periods start. Sean goes on the day his spousal visa comes through. Mishka alone: Momentum Incentive R3,627 a month, or Discovery Classic Smart R3,257.

⏳ Waiting periods, in plain words

  • 3 months: new members with no previous medical aid wait up to 3 months before claims are paid.
  • Up to 12 months: the scheme can exclude anything you were treated for in the last 12 months, for up to a year.
  • Pregnancy: if you're already pregnant when you join, the scheme can refuse to pay for it. A pregnancy that starts after joining is covered. So join first, and get the waiting periods in writing before planning anything.
  • Answer every question on the form honestly. If you leave something out, they can cancel the cover later, exactly when you need it.
