How the deposit works: you float R45,000 by week 36. For a normal birth the actual costs (R18k + R2.8k paed + any extras) are deducted and the balance (~R24,000) is refunded within a week of going home. The deposit is really there to cover an emergency C-section (anaesthetist ~R8k + paed ~R4.4k paid from it). Your gynae (if used) bills separately.
