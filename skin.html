Skin reset.

Keep it gentle now. Build slowly after the course, and track what actually changes.

🧴 Now: on Roaccutane, keep it simple

Current course: 20 mg. The active-product plan below is on hold.

  • Gentle cleanser.
  • Fragrance-free moisturiser, several times a day.
  • SPF 50+ every morning, with lip balm throughout the day.

Stop: COSRX BHA, PFB Vanish, benzoyl peroxide, glycolic/AHA, Skinoren, retinal/adapalene, Medicube pads and minoxidil.

Hold Anua + azelaic acid until the rash has fully settled. Check reintroduction with the prescriber and stop if they sting.

Tell the prescriber about the rash. Only change the dose with them.

🌱 After the course: restart at week 0

Confirm with your prescriber when your skin is ready. One new product every 2 weeks, only if the previous one is comfortable.

  1. Week 0: SPF every morning + The Ordinary Azelaic Acid 10% in the morning. Keep cleanser and moisturiser. Take baseline photos.
  2. Week 2: Anua Niacinamide + TXA serum in the morning; PFB Vanish on the beard line after trims.
  3. Week 4: add COSRX BHA, Monday and Thursday nights.
  4. Week 6: add one retinoid, Tuesday and Saturday nights. A pea-sized amount, sandwiched between moisturiser layers.

These are earliest checkpoints, not deadlines. If SPF or both week-2 products are new, introduce them separately, 2 weeks apart, and move later additions back. Never rush to catch up.

Azelaic step-up: only consider Skinoren 15% after 6 weeks on 10% with no stinging. Make it a separate change.

Morning, once introduced: cleanser → Anua → azelaic → SPF last.

🌙 One night schedule

Only after each product has been introduced and tolerated.

Mon + Thu: cleanser → COSRX BHA → moisturiser.

Tue + Sat: cleanser → moisturiser → retinoid → moisturiser.

Wed / Fri / Sun: cleanser → moisturiser only.

After trims: use PFB only on a separate night, replacing a moisturiser-only night. Never use it on a BHA or retinoid night. Otherwise skip it.

Never stack acids or combine them with the retinoid. Do not add the PDF's extra acid nights.

PDF had two versions of the nights; I used the one that never stacks acids.

📸 Does it help?

Take baseline photos of both cheeks, then every 4 weeks. Same light, angle and distance, no filter. Keep the photos privately on your phone; this page stores checks and notes only.

Choose the date when you actually restart. Check dates follow it; changing the date keeps your existing checks and notes.

    🛡️ Rules + stop signals

    • No picking or squeezing. It can leave new dark marks.
    • Trim, don't shave close. Use a short guard.
    • SPF always. Every morning, including near windows; reapply when outdoors.
    • Patch test for 3 days on a small area under the jaw before each new product.
    • Stop signals: burning, peeling patches, stinging that lasts more than a few minutes, or redness into the next morning. Stop that product for a week. Once settled, discuss restarting at half the frequency; persistent irritation needs your prescriber.
    • Avoid unregulated lightening creams. Ask the prescriber how long to wait before laser or clinic procedures after Roaccutane.

    See a GP: bumps become hard and raised, pus-filled bumps spread, or nothing changes by week 12. Tell your Roaccutane prescriber about the current rash now.

    Urgent help: a blistering or peeling rash, mouth sores, or facial swelling / trouble breathing needs immediate medical help. For a serious rash, stop Roaccutane and contact a doctor immediately. Isotretinoin patient leaflet.
