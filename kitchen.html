🍳 Starter kitchen (25 Oct): pots, plates, cutlery, glasses

Value kitchen: ~R8,240 (all the kitchen cards below, not counting the blenders). Step-up (Le Creuset, Tramontina, Corelle, 18/10 cutlery): ~R20,700. Spend on: the knife and the pots, because you use them every day. Save on: plates, glasses and cutlery. Corelle can wait until the toddler throws things. Le Creuset (R7,699) mostly buys a lifetime warranty. The Sagenwolf does the same soups at a fifth of the price. Get a stockpot for bone broth: a 5L cast-iron pot full of broth weighs ~9kg and is too small for bones.

Buy for move day (~R4.7k): pot set, plates, cutlery, glasses, both knives, board, spoons. Later: glass containers, Dutch oven, skillet, stockpot. Several Takealot items say 5–7 work days, so order by ~27 Oct to have them by 1 Nov. Cast iron works on any stove; on glass-ceramic, lift it, don't slide it.

🍲 Blender shortlist (25 Oct): picked for reliability, not watts

The honest take: for soups, broth soups, sauces and baby purée, a good stick blender is the best tool. You blend right in the pot, so there's no pouring of hot liquid, and baby portions are too small for a 2L jug. Add a jug blender only if smoothies or frozen fruit matter. Dropped: Genesis Nutrimax (Verimark brand, ~1.5★ on Hello Peter, the 2200W can't be verified) and the Russell Hobbs Nexus. Vitamix is the only true buy-it-for-life brand, but it's ~R12.7k in SA, out of stock, and has no local refurb programme.
